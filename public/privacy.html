Your information stays on this device

FindAgain by Xavier Wang. Updated October 24, 2026.

Item names, saved places and photos are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB on your computer or phone. The app does not upload them to a server. It does not require an account and does not sync records between devices or browsers.

Photos are resized on your device. Search happens on your device. Voice typing is optional and starts only when you tap a microphone button. Your browser may send audio to its speech service for transcription under that provider’s privacy policy. Spoken item names or places are therefore shared with that service. FindAgain does not record or store audio. Review the transcribed words before saving; saved records and photos remain in this browser. Read-aloud is available only when your browser reports an installed, on-device voice.

Anyone who can use this browser profile can see its saved items. This app does not add a separate password or encrypt its browser database. Use your device’s screen lock and your own browser profile.

Removing an item deletes its saved place and photo from this browser. Clearing site data, using private browsing, uninstalling a browser, or browser storage cleanup may erase records. There is no automatic cloud backup. Export backup downloads a file containing your items and photos; Restore backup imports missing items from that file on this device. Existing item names are left unchanged. Backup files are not encrypted. Keep them private, and choose local storage if you do not want your device to save them to a cloud drive.

The website itself is delivered by ChatGPT Sites. Its hosting provider receives ordinary page and asset requests, such as your IP address, but the app does not include your saved item details in those requests. Device or browser backup software may separately back up local browser data according to your settings.
